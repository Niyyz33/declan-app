Get into the phone fast ⚡

The quickest way in, with no Apple ID.

This only works on a phone that’s been wiped and signed out. If it shows Activation Lock or asks for the seller’s Apple ID, don’t try to get round it. Get them to unlock it. If they can’t, don’t buy it.
You never sign in to an Apple ID. You skip it. And never sign in to your own Apple ID on a phone you’re checking.
1

Turn on your hotspot

A wiped phone has no Wi-Fi, and it needs internet to switch on. Use yours.

On your phone: Settings › Personal Hotspot, then turn on Allow Others to Join. Stay on that screen until the other phone connects. Have your hotspot password ready.

2

Is it wiped?

If you see the “hello” screen, it’s wiped. Go to the next step.

If it shows the Home Screen, ask the seller to erase it in front of you:

Settings › General › Transfer or Reset iPhone › Erase All Content and Settings

They’ll type their passcode and Apple ID password. That’s normal. It also switches off Find My.

3

Skip through the set-up

Tap through in this order. The button you want is nearly always Set Up Later, Don’t … or Skip.

1
Pick the language and country (Australia).
2
If it offers Quick Start, tap Set Up Manually (or Set Up Without Another Device).
3
Wi-Fi: tap your hotspot and type the password. You can’t skip this one.
4
Wait for “Activating iPhone”. If it says Activation Lock, stop and get them to unlock it.
5
Data & Privacy: tap Continue.
6
Face ID: tap Set Up Later.
7
Passcode: tap Passcode Options, then Don’t Use Passcode.
8
Transfer Your Apps & Data: tap Don’t Transfer Apps & Data.
9
Apple Account: tap Forgot password or don’t have an Apple ID?, then Set Up Later in Settings.
10
Terms and Conditions: tap Agree.
11
Any other screen (Siri, Screen Time, analytics): tap Set Up Later, Continue or Don’t Share.
12
Welcome to iPhone: tap Get Started. You’re in.

The screens move around a little between iOS versions, and the exact words can change. If you’re unsure, pick the one that means later or skip.

Stuck?

📶
Your hotspot doesn’t show up. Stay on your Personal Hotspot screen, hold the phones close together, and turn Airplane Mode off.
🔁
It keeps asking for an Apple ID. Tap Don’t Use or Set Up Later. If it won’t go past, it may be locked. Get the seller to unlock it.
🔔
“Finish Setting Up Your iPhone” banner. Ignore it.
4

The first minute inside

Go to Settings › General › About and check:

1
Model Name matches the lead. If it doesn’t, the quote changes. Tell the seller politely. Note the Capacity (storage) too, you’ll need it when you log the phone.
2
Carrier Lock says No SIM restrictions.
3
Scroll down to Parts and Service History. Look for Unknown Part.
Next, check the battery.
5

Check the battery health

Straight after About, go to Settings › Battery › Battery Health & Charging.

Look at Maximum Capacity. Under 85% is a deduction. Under 80% you always deduct it. The amount is in the inspection guide.

6

Test Face ID

Go to Settings › Face ID & Passcode and tap Set Up Face ID. If the camera starts scanning your face, it works. It may ask you to make a temporary passcode first, which is fine.

If it can’t set up, or the camera won’t scan, that’s a deduction.

7

When you’re done

Turn your hotspot off.

Leave the phone as it is. Never sign in to your own Apple ID on it.
