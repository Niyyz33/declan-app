If something goes wrong 🛠️

Don’t panic. Here’s what to do.

Never call Ethan during a meetup. Everything you need is on the meetup card. If you’re not sure, walk away and text him afterwards.
1

At a meetup

⏱
Seller doesn’t turn up. Wait a few minutes. Then open the Meetups tab and tap No show.
⏻
The phone doesn’t turn on. Don’t buy it.
🔒
It asks for the seller’s Apple ID. Don’t buy it.
💬
Seller wants more than the quote. Be friendly and say no. If they won’t budge, walk away.
🚶
Something feels off. Walk away. Text Ethan afterwards.
2

In the app

📶
No signal. Keep going. Phones save on your phone and send by themselves when the signal’s back.
🔑
Can’t log in. Text Ethan. He can reset your password.
🔴
Red bar: “couldn’t be sent”. Tap it, then tap Try again.
🔔
No alerts. Open Settings › Notifications › Cash for Phones and make sure they’re on. Also make sure you use the app from its Home Screen icon.
📅
Calendar’s behind. It can take a while. Check the Meetups tab, which is always up to date.
🔄
Something looks off or a tab’s missing. Swipe the app away, then open it again.
✏️
I logged a phone wrong. Go to Phones, then Edit or Delete. You can do that until it’s shipped.
💬

Still stuck?

Text Ethan after the meetup. He’ll sort it.
