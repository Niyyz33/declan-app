What to say 💬

The same easy flow, every meetup.

Everything you need is on your meetup card: the seller’s name, the model, the issues and the quote. Look at it before you go.
1

Say hi

Hey, how are you? I’m Declan, nice to meet you.
2

Check out the phone

Ask to have a look at the phone.

Get into it, without signing in to an Apple ID. Check everything, and keep the deductions in your head while you go. Use the inspection guide, in order.

Open the inspection guide →
3

Say your deductions

After you’ve been through the whole phone, say it like this. Swap the green bits for what you actually found.

All right, so from having a look at the phone, obviously we’ve got the cracked back, which you’ve already talked about.

However, what I wasn’t aware of is the battery is below 85% and will need to be replaced, so that is a deduction.

As well as that, there are light scratches on the screen, and for me to sell that, I’ll need to replace it.

So with the deductions, I’d be at $X. $X is the quote minus your deductions.
Be friendly, be honest, and say the number once.
4

Pay and finish

Hand over the cash, say thanks, and you’re done.

Log the phone after, when you’re on your own. Don’t use the app while you’re with the seller.
