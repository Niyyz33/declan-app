Mint phones 💚

Phones with nothing wrong. These ones are yours to sell.

1

What is a mint phone?

A phone with nothing wrong with it, or just a worn battery.

You keep it and sell it yourself. It doesn’t get shipped to Ethan.

2

Where they come from

Ethan sends you the leads. When Ethan sends you a lead, that is your go-ahead to buy it. You don’t need to ask.

Buy it the same way as any other phone. See what to say.

3

Log it when you buy it

Tap Log, go through the phone, and set the type to Mint. Do it after the meetup, as always.

4

Sell it on Marketplace

Sell your mint phones on Facebook Marketplace.

5

Record what it sold for

When it sells:

1
Open the Phones tab and find the phone. It says With you.
2
Tap 💰 Mark sold.
3
Type what it sold for and tap Save.

Got it wrong? Tap Change sale price. If the sale fell through, tap It didn’t sell after all.

6

Ethan’s commission

Ethan takes a commission on each mint phone. He’ll go through it with you.
