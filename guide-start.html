Welcome aboard! 👋

Set your app up in 5 minutes. You only do this once.

1

Add it to your Home Screen

Open the link Ethan sent you in Safari.

Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow) at the bottom. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

From now on, always open the app from its icon on your Home Screen, never from Safari. That is what keeps you logged in and stops you ever losing a phone you logged.
2

Log in

Open the app from the icon and log in with the email and password Ethan gave you.

You stay logged in, even when you have no signal.

Log in from the icon, not from Safari. They are separate, so logging in on Safari does nothing for the Home Screen app.
3

Turn on alerts

Go to the Meetups tab. Tap Turn on alerts, then Allow.

You get a buzz the moment Ethan books you a meetup, and another one an hour before it starts.

4

Add the calendar

In the Meetups tab, tap Add to Apple Calendar, then Subscribe.

Every meetup Ethan books now shows up in your calendar by itself.

Your calendar can take a little while to catch up, so for anything booked last minute, check the Meetups tab first.
5

Your five tabs

🏠
Home
What’s next, and how close you are to 5 damaged phones.
📅
Meetups
Everything for each meetup: the seller’s name, the model, the issues, the quote and where.
＋
Log
Log a phone after you’ve bought it.
📱
Phones
Everything you’ve bought. Fix a mistake, or mark a mint phone as sold.
📦
Ship
Message Ethan for a label and follow your parcel.
6

Your first phone

Log it after the sale, not during. Don’t use the app while you’re with the seller.

On the Meetups tab, tap Log this phone on the meetup. The model and quote fill in for you.

No signal? Keep going. It saves on your phone and sends by itself. Closed the app halfway? Your phone is saved. Open the app and tap Continue.

✓

All set?
