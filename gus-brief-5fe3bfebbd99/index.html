3

Set up your camera first

1
Open the Camera app and switch to Video.
2
Tap or flick open the camera controls at the top of the screen.
3
Check the video resolution and set it to the highest available (usually 4K).
4
Check the frame rate and set it to the highest available (usually 60).
Do this before the first video and check it again if you restart the Camera app. Make sure you have enough storage space. High settings make bigger files.
4

Good light, every video

Do thisLight in front of you, face clear
Not thisWindow behind you, face dark
☀️
Use daylight where you can. Face the light.
👀
Your face, the phone and the cash must all be easy to see.
🚫
No dark rooms and no strong light behind you.
Good lighting is one of the main priorities. A new background doesn’t help if the video is too dark.
5

Keep it quiet

People need to hear you clearly. Avoid:

🚗Traffic💨Strong wind🗣️People talking📺TV🎵Music🔊Loud appliances

If a spot is noisy, move somewhere quieter. Record a few seconds first and play it back to check.

6

Framing

1
Show your face, the phone, box or cash, and enough background to see where you are.
2
Props must not block your face.
3
Keep the camera steady.

When all 21 are done: send them to Ethan

Don’t send them on WhatsApp, iMessage, Instagram or normal email. Those shrink the video and the quality is lost.
Use SwissTransfer. It’s free, you don’t need an account, and it sends the original file at full quality. It can send up to 50 GB at once, so all 21 videos are no problem.

Send straight from your iPhone

1
Save the videos as files. In Photos, tap Select, pick your 21 videos, tap the Share button, choose Save to Files and make a new folder called Ads. This keeps the original full-quality files.
2
Install the free app Infomaniak SwissTransfer from the App Store. You don’t need to sign up or log in.
3
Open it, add the videos from the Ads folder in Files, and choose to send by email.
4
Put ethan.lancuba@gmail.com as the person receiving it, put your own email as the sender, and set it to stay available for 30 days.
5
Tap Transfer. It emails you a code to check it’s really your address. Check your spam if it doesn’t show up. Type the code in within 15 minutes.
6
Stay on Wi-Fi and keep the app open until it says it’s done. Then text Ethan “sent”.
I get an email with the download link when it’s done. Stuck on any step? Text Ethan, but have a read here first.
